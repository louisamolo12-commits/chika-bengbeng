Crossfire

Crossfire is a fast-paced multiplayer first-person shooter game developed by Smilegate Entertainment. The game features a variety of modes, including the iconic Counter-Terrorist vs. Terrorist gameplay, and is known for its wide variety of weapons, strategic gameplay, and intense team-based combat.

In Crossfire, players take on the role of elite mercenaries fighting in global hot spots. They can choose from different factions, including the Global Risk and the Black List, each with their unique abilities and weapon choices.
